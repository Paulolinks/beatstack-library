# Beat Stack Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brasa Builders ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use Beat Stack Library, Beat Stack Manager, related software, websites, services, updates, and cloud features (collectively, the "Software" or "Service").

By using the Software, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Software, we may collect the following information:

### Account Information

- Name
- Email address
- Username
- Password or authentication credentials
- License key
- Subscription or purchase status

### Payment Information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment processors. We may receive payment status, transaction ID, billing email, plan type, and purchase history. We do not intentionally store full credit card numbers on our own servers.

### User Content

If you use Beat Stack Library or cloud features, you may upload or store audio files, beats, samples, loops, presets, packs, folders, and related files.

We may also store metadata connected to your files, including file names, folder names, tags, BPM, key, ratings, favorites, notes, upload dates, file size, file type, and library organization data.

### Device and Usage Information

We may collect limited technical information such as IP address, device type, operating system, app version, login date and time, error logs, crash reports, feature usage, and security logs.

### Support Information

If you contact support, we may collect your name, email address, message, screenshots, files you choose to send, and information needed to respond to your request.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and manage accounts
- Provide access to the Software
- Store and organize your Beat Stack Library
- Sync cloud-based content when applicable
- Process purchases, licenses, and subscriptions
- Provide customer support
- Improve app performance and reliability
- Fix bugs and security issues
- Prevent fraud, abuse, piracy, unauthorized access, and illegal activity
- Enforce our Terms of Use
- Respond to legal requests, copyright notices, or law enforcement requests when required

## 3. User Content Ownership

You keep ownership of your User Content.

We do not claim ownership of your beats, samples, loops, presets, packs, or audio files.

We only process, store, and access User Content as needed to provide, maintain, secure, support, or improve the Software, or as required by law or legal claims.

## 4. Local vs Cloud Use

Beat Stack Manager may store data locally on your device unless you enable cloud, sync, backup, account, update, license verification, or support features.

Beat Stack Library may store your files and metadata on servers, VPS infrastructure, cloud storage, databases, or third-party providers so you can access your library remotely.

## 5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information with hosting providers, VPS providers, cloud storage providers, database providers, payment processors, email providers, analytics or crash reporting tools, security and fraud prevention providers, customer support tools, and legal, government, or copyright authorities when required.

These providers may only access information needed to perform services for us.

## 6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, such as access controls, authentication, server security, and limited access to personal information.

However, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, data loss, corruption, downtime, or security incidents will never occur.

You are responsible for keeping backup copies of your important files and protecting your login credentials.

## 7. Data Retention

We keep personal information and User Content for as long as needed to provide the Software, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud, and maintain business records.

If you delete your account or request deletion, we will make reasonable efforts to delete or anonymize your personal information and User Content, unless we need to keep certain information for legal, security, backup, accounting, fraud prevention, or legitimate business reasons.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information we have about you.
